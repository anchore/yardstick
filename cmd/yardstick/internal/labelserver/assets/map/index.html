Reading the map

The gate is drawn first: one bar per check, named above it. The shaded range runs from the worst case for the candidate to the best ("worst 14 … 2 best"), since the unlabeled deltas could still go either way; the amber line is the threshold, labeled with the side that passes ("pass if ≤ 0"). A range entirely on the passing side is a pass, entirely on the failing side a fail, and one that straddles the line is undecided: labeling narrows it. Hovering an "accept N ✦" control draws a dashed range where the bar would be if those agent hints were accepted.

Then the images, as small multiples: each a bar of its differences by label (● TP ○ FP ◐ unclear, the empty track unlabeled). Click one to look at that image only.

The map is the differences by area: + candidate added on the left, − candidate dropped on the right, each divided by the computed reason the two sides differ, then by source package when you zoom in. A block's fill is its labeled share in the label colours; the hatched part has an agent's suggestion; the rest is open. ◆ marks blocks that hold lines that could change the gate's verdict. Click a block to zoom into it; click a package block to label its findings on the tile.

On a gate the first picture is the board: one panel per check, what decides it, and the open findings that bear on it grouped by how many labels one action settles. Click a group (or a failed check's claims) and the page becomes the focus tile for one finding of that group, with a breadcrumb back: ← gate board › the check › the group · 3 of 46. The breadcrumb (or Esc) is the way back. Labeling moves to the next unlabeled match in the group, unless you turn that off on the tile; when the group is done, the tile offers the next group of the same check.

✦ researched marks matches research-vuln has run on, however it was started (the label UI, a session); ✦ triaged marks matches with only a triage-deltas hint. A match shows its highest level; hover a mark for which skill and who asked. Above the board, "research N matches that could move a check" and "… with no agent hint" send an agent to the next few of those that aren't researched yet (the first also takes triaged ones), in every image or the one in scope.
